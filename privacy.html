Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Flyby Reports keeps your work on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive your photos, reports or account details, and we do not sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Jobs, photos, videos, reports, scanned documents and voice transcripts are stored on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • Location is used to tag photos with where they were taken and to show which job you are at. It is stored with the photo on your phone.
  • Camera and microphone are used only when you take photos, record video or dictate.
  • Speech-to-text and report writing run on the phone using Apple's on-device features.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for permission to create and manage only the files and folders it creates itself (the drive.file permission), and to see your email address so it can show which account is connected.

  • The app uploads copies of your photos, videos, report PDFs and scanned documents to a "Flyby Reports" folder in your own Google Drive. You can also ask it to make an editable Google Doc copy of a report.
  • The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Uploads go directly from your phone to Google. They do not pass through any server of ours.
  • The sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. Your Google password is never seen by the app.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to anyone else, and do not use it to train AI models.

Flyby Reports' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Deleting a photo, report or job in the app removes it from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Files already copied to Google Drive stay there until you delete them in Drive. The app never deletes files in your Drive.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive in the app's Account tab at any time, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

The app is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
