Flyby Reports

Job site photos and reports for contractors, kept apart from your personal photos.

Flyby Reports is an iPhone app for people who inspect and document job sites. Take photos and video, mark them up, talk through what you see, and turn it into a report you can send as a PDF.

  • Work photos stay in the app, sorted by job, not in your camera roll.
  • Each photo records when and where it was taken.
  • Reports are written on the phone from what you say and capture.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, in a folder per job.

Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive, Flyby Reports copies your photos, videos, report PDFs and scanned documents to a "Flyby Reports" folder in your own Drive. The app can only see the files it created there. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.
